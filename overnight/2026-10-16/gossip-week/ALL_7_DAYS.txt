DAY 1: THE WEIGHT OF WORDS

Somebody told you something about somebody this week, and you passed it on. You thought it was small.

Proverbs 18:21: "Death and life are in the power of the tongue: and they that love it shall eat the fruit thereof."

Death and life. Your mouth is not a harmless little thing. It works like a firearm in one way: once the round leaves the barrel, you can say sorry all day and the round keeps going. You can apologize to the person. You cannot call it back.

Watch the end of that verse. They that love it shall eat the fruit. Whatever you plant with your mouth, you end up eating. The word you spoke over somebody else's life grows, and one day you're sitting at the table with it.

So before you say what you heard, ask what you're planting. Then ask if you want to eat it.

Drop an amen if that landed. Have a blessed day, and remember the Word for today.


DAY 2: TWO IN AGREEMENT

Strike a tuning fork. Put a second fork across the room, same note, and don't touch it. It starts ringing on its own. Nobody hit it. It just matched.

That's agreement. Matthew 18:19: "Again I say unto you, That if two of you shall agree on earth as touching any thing that they shall ask, it shall be done for them of my Father which is in heaven."

The Greek word for agree there is where we get the word symphony. Two people on the same note, and heaven pays attention. We love that verse at prayer meeting.

Now say something ugly about your coworker, and watch the person next to you say, "Yeah, I knew it." You just struck a fork. They started ringing. Two people, one note, and the note is somebody's downfall.

Agreement is a force, and it doesn't care which direction you point it.

Hit like if you're pointing yours somewhere better. Have a blessed day.


DAY 3: THE ACCUSER NEEDS A WITNESS

The devil has a job. Revelation 12:10 calls him the accuser of our brethren, who accused them before our God day and night. Day and night. That's his whole career.

But an accuser needs evidence. A prosecutor with no witness loses the case.

So here you come. Break room, parking lot, group chat. You tell what you heard about her marriage. Somebody says, "I knew it." You two just agreed, and the accuser was standing right there taking notes.

You didn't pass along news. You took the stand, and he didn't have to pay you a dime.

Ephesians 4:27: "Neither give place to the devil." Place means ground. A word spoken in agreement over somebody is ground you handed him, and it was never yours to hand over. That person belongs to God.

The word Paul uses for whisperers in Romans 1:29 comes from the murmur of a snake charmer. Next time you lean in close to say something low, listen to what you sound like.

Follow for more. Have a blessed day, and remember the Word for today.


DAY 4: JUDGED WHILE YOU'RE NOT IN THE ROOM

A man gets a letter. The court date was last Tuesday and judgment is already entered against him. He never got the summons, so he never showed up, so nobody spoke for him. That's called a default judgment, and it's how gossip works.

Isaiah 54:17: "No weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper; and every tongue that shall rise against thee in judgment thou shalt condemn."

God gave His people the right to condemn the tongue that rises against them. But you can only answer what you've heard. The man you're talking about never heard it. Nobody told him there was a hearing, and he got sentenced anyway, by people who'll shake his hand Sunday and ask how the family is doing.

When somebody's whole week falls apart for no reason you can see, some of it may have been decided in a room they were never invited into.

Don't sit on that jury. Save this one. Have a blessed day, and remember the Word for today.


DAY 5: GOD HEARD IT

Miriam and Aaron talked about their brother Moses. It was family business, and they said it quietly, away from him. Numbers 12:2: "And they said, Hath the LORD indeed spoken only by Moses? hath he not spoken also by us? And the LORD heard it."

And the LORD heard it. Moses never said a word. He didn't defend himself and he didn't call a meeting. God came down on His own, and Miriam walked out of that tent leprous, white as snow.

In plain terms: you talked about a man's son while his father stood right behind you. The man himself may never find out. His Father already did.

Everybody you've ever talked about has a Father who was listening, and He doesn't wait for them to complain.

Send this to someone who needs it before they open their mouth. Have a blessed day.


DAY 6: WOOD AND FIRE

A fire needs fuel. Proverbs 26:20: "Where no wood is, there the fire goeth out: so where there is no talebearer, the strife ceaseth." Take the wood away and the fire dies on its own.

James 3:5: "Even so the tongue is a little member, and boasteth great things. Behold, how great a matter a little fire kindleth!"

So when somebody hands you a story about another person, you're standing in a bucket line. The first man pours gasoline, and every man after him is passing the bucket toward somebody's house. You didn't start it. You helped it get there.

You know the old story about the man who cut a pillow open on a windy day and then tried to gather the feathers? Everybody laughs at that man, then goes right back to passing feathers.

When the bucket gets to you, set it down. A fire nobody feeds goes out by itself.

Share this with somebody who'd rather put the fire out. Have a blessed day, and remember the Word for today.


DAY 7: IF WE CAN TALK ABOUT THEM, WE CAN PRAY FOR THEM

If you've got the time to talk about somebody's trouble, you've got the time to pray over it.

James 5:16: "Confess your faults one to another, and pray one for another, that ye may be healed."

Same mouth, same breath, same two people already standing there. You already called the meeting. You just put the wrong name on the agenda. And that agreement works both ways. Two people agreeing for somebody carries weight in heaven too.

So here's what I want you to carry out the door. If we can talk about them, we can pray for them.

Next time you hear your own voice halfway through a sentence about somebody, let the Holy Spirit stop you right there and pray for that person. And tell whoever you're with: that one is a child of God too. Romans 8:16: "The Spirit itself beareth witness with our spirit, that we are the children of God." Their name is in the same book as yours.

Every idle word gets accounted for, Matthew 12:36. Let's make ours worth something.

Drop an amen. Have a blessed day, and remember the Word for today.
