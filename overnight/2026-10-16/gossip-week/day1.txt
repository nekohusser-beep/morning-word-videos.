DAY 1: THE WEIGHT OF WORDS

Somebody told you something about somebody this week, and you passed it on. You thought it was small.

Proverbs 18:21: "Death and life are in the power of the tongue: and they that love it shall eat the fruit thereof."

Death and life. Your mouth is not a harmless little thing. It works like a firearm in one way: once the round leaves the barrel, you can say sorry all day and the round keeps going. You can apologize to the person. You cannot call it back.

Watch the end of that verse. They that love it shall eat the fruit. Whatever you plant with your mouth, you end up eating. The word you spoke over somebody else's life grows, and one day you're sitting at the table with it.

So before you say what you heard, ask what you're planting. Then ask if you want to eat it.

Drop an amen if that landed. Have a blessed day, and remember the Word for today.
