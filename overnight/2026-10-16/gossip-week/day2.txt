DAY 2: TWO IN AGREEMENT

Strike a tuning fork. Put a second fork across the room, same note, and don't touch it. It starts ringing on its own. Nobody hit it. It just matched.

That's agreement. Matthew 18:19: "Again I say unto you, That if two of you shall agree on earth as touching any thing that they shall ask, it shall be done for them of my Father which is in heaven."

The Greek word for agree there is where we get the word symphony. Two people on the same note, and heaven pays attention. We love that verse at prayer meeting.

Now say something ugly about your coworker, and watch the person next to you say, "Yeah, I knew it." You just struck a fork. They started ringing. Two people, one note, and the note is somebody's downfall.

Agreement is a force, and it doesn't care which direction you point it.

Hit like if you're pointing yours somewhere better. Have a blessed day.
