DAY 5: GOD HEARD IT

Miriam and Aaron talked about their brother Moses. It was family business, and they said it quietly, away from him. Numbers 12:2: "And they said, Hath the LORD indeed spoken only by Moses? hath he not spoken also by us? And the LORD heard it."

And the LORD heard it. Moses never said a word. He didn't defend himself and he didn't call a meeting. God came down on His own, and Miriam walked out of that tent leprous, white as snow.

In plain terms: you talked about a man's son while his father stood right behind you. The man himself may never find out. His Father already did.

Everybody you've ever talked about has a Father who was listening, and He doesn't wait for them to complain.

Send this to someone who needs it before they open their mouth. Have a blessed day.
