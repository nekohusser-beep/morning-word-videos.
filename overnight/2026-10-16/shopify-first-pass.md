# Shopify first pass (night-shift sleep niche)

The night-shift niche has real competitors to study. Manta Sleep sells blackout masks and bundles aimed at night-shift workers, with side-sleep comfort and blackout stickers for LED lights. Verified reviews of Blackout EZ window covers praise how well they block light. One mask maker's reviews show the main complaint: the material doesn't actually block light.

Candidates, kept to the light, small items that suit dropshipping:
1. A true-blackout 3D sleep mask, fixing the light-leak complaint.
2. LED blackout stickers as a cheap add-on.
3. A "Night Shift Survival Kit" bundle (mask, stickers, earplugs, door sign), framed as a Christmas gift for plant workers and nurses.

Window covers are too bulky to dropship. Sleep patches carry health-claim risk, so I'd skip them.

On suppliers, CJ is mixed to positive. It has a 4.9 on the Shopify App Store but a Trustpilot score suppressed after fake reviews were removed, and its shipping and agent support are hit-or-miss. Most of this came from listicle sites, so treat it as direction, not proof. The real vetting needs CJ's actual product pages and order samples, which wait on the browser.
