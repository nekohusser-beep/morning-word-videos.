# Long-video SEO standard (Blessed Prayers) - Nov 10 2026

## What we found (Studio, Nov 10 2026 10:40 PM ET)
- Metadata is NOT the gap. Sleep readings already have 20 tags, English, a long description, playlist links and book playlists.
- The gap is reach. Psalms 8 Hr 49 Min: 157 impressions in 27 days, CTR 9.6%, 29:55 avg view (the best long video, 50 views).
  Psalms 1-31 (Oct 29): 18 impressions in 12 days; its views came from the playlist and Related Shorts only.
- ADHD long (Nov 10): YouTube DID test it, 910 impressions in a day, but CTR 1.0% and 0:19 avg view. Thumbnail/title and first 30 s are the problem there, not tags.
- Shorts get pushed by the Shorts feed to people who never searched. Long videos only get found through search, suggested and browse, and YouTube only grows that after viewers click and stay.

## Why (in order of size)
1. Bulk dumps: ~25 long videos published Oct 29, ~10 Oct 22-23, 3 Oct 19. YouTube splits a small channel's test impressions across all of them, so each one gets almost none.
2. Duplicates: Matthew 1-17, Matthew 18-28, Revelation, James/Eph/Phil and Gospel of Matthew are each up twice (Ocean Waves and No Music), and every chunk repeats a full-book upload. They compete with each other.
3. Wrong category: new long uploads were landing in Comedy (both Nov 11 Heaven videos were; fixed to People & Blogs Nov 10 10:50 PM ET).
4. No bridge from Shorts: only the Psalms readings had Shorts pointing at them (Related video). That is the one source of long-video views that is working.
5. Missing extras vs. the Shorts treatment: no pinned comment, no end screen, no chapters, prayer videos not in a playlist.

## The standard for every long video from now on
Before upload (meta JSON): search-phrase title + verse + KJV (same formula as Shorts), 12-20 tags, categoryId 22 (People & Blogs; 23 Comedy only for Skip), first description line = the search phrase, KJV verse word for word, chapters (0:00 ...) for any video over 8 minutes.
Publishing: one long video per day max. No second version of the same reading (no "No Music" twin). Publish at the slot (5 AM morning, 8:30 PM night).
Studio finish (nightly task, Part B): category, language, tags, playlist, save; pinned comment once public.
Shorts bridge: every long video gets at least one Short whose Related video is that long video, published the same day.
Thumbnail: CTR under 4% after 1,000 impressions = new thumbnail (ADHD long is at 1.0%).
