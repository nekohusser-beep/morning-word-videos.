# Ask Studio (YouTube's AI) channel diagnosis - Nov 17 2026, Blessed Prayers

Asked: last 10 Shorts + 28 days of long videos: what underperformed and why, better titles/thumbnails for top 3 and bottom 3, what to change in the first 3 seconds.

## Long videos (28 days)
Top: What It's Like to Have ADHD (Animated) | Meet Skip 139 views, 1.51% CTR, 19.8% ret / Is Hell Real? The Part Nobody Tells You 107, 1.68%, 19.8% / The 21 Martyrs of Libya 35, 4.41% CTR, 45.1% ret.

## Shorts
- End Times Short: 75% stayed (best hook) but retention fell to 14.8% (24 s of 2:47): viewers wanted the answer fast.
- Rapture Short: 69.7% stayed, 44.7% retention (best overall).
- ADHD Trying to Focus: most views (149), 44.3% stayed.
- Weak: Psalm 121 sleep Short 25% stayed; Philippians 4:6-7 anxiety Short 37.9% stayed.
- Why people swipe: slow, passive openings with no motion and no urgent first line, lost in the first 1-2 s.

## Why the long videos underperformed
- Titles read like a church directory "(1 Corinthians 10:13)" instead of the viewer's pain. 0% CTR on two.
- Money prayer lost people in the first 30 s: slow build before the help.
- 21 Martyrs worked: high stakes at 0:01 ("every one of them had a chance to walk away").

## First 3 seconds of every Short (YouTube's fix)
1. No dead air: first spoken syllable on frame 1. (The Rapture Short's speech started at second 5.)
2. Open with the tension or the dilemma, never a greeting or soft music intro. Example: "If you wake up at 3 AM with your mind racing, stop and listen to this verse."
3. Bold on-screen text in the top-center third that changes every 1-1.5 s.
4. Every spoken word in the first 3 s gets a visual change (punch-in zoom, cut, animated text).
