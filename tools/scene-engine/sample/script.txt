Most mornings I grab my phone before I grab my Bible. I check the news, the bills, the messages, and by the time my feet hit the floor I am already tired. Then I read one old verse and the whole day changes. Psalm 23:1 says, "The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want." A shepherd walks ahead of the sheep and knows where the water is. The sheep do not need a map. They need to stay close. That is a lot easier than what I was doing, which was trying to be my own shepherd before breakfast. So the plan is not a bigger morning routine. The plan is one verse. Pick it, read it slowly, and let it be the first voice you hear. The Lord who walked ahead of David is the same Lord who walks ahead of you.
