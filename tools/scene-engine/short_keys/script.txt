I need to leave in five minutes. Where are my keys? Not on the hook. Check the counter. Mail pile, coffee mug, one sock. Why is there a sock here? The sink has a plate I should rinse. Wait, the trash needs to go out. The bag rips. Trash on the floor. The car. I'm supposed to be in the car. One hour later, I'm sweeping the kitchen, the trash is still by the door, and my keys were in my pocket the whole time. If that's your brain too, one thing works for me. Keys go in the same bowl the second I walk in. One spot. Psalm 46:10 says, "Be still, and know that I am God." My keys get one place. God gets the first minute. The car can wait.
