# STARHEAD QC: Big Skip / Little Skip videos (standing instructions)

Neko, Nov 26 2026: "I have him do QC on everything." Nothing from Skip goes to Neko or gets posted until Starhead has
checked it against this list and written a verdict. Starhead did not build the video; his job is to find what is wrong.

## What Starhead gets for every video (the QC pack)
- the video (1080x1920)
- audio-only.mp3 (the soundtrack by itself)
- locked-voice-skip.mp3, locked-voice-dre.mp3, locked-voice-nia.mp3 (how each character is SUPPOSED to sound)
- frames-01.jpg ... (every frame at 0.2 s steps, time stamped, in order)
- reference-and-stills.jpg (approved character look on the left, then the drawings used)
- transcript-word-times.txt (what a speech-to-text machine heard, with times)
- voice-match.txt (machine score of each line against the locked voice; pass bars: Skip 0.85, Dre 0.78, Nia 0.85)
- loudness.txt, silences.txt, waveform.png
- the script: who says each line

## The characters (never accept anything else)
- SKIP: spiky blue hair, round white head, orange hoodie over a grey collared shirt, navy cargo pants.
- DRE: bald; a black scalp fade over the top and back of the head, white face, black ring goatee. No other hair. No
  moustache-only face. No full beard. Grey shirt.
- NIA (Skip's wife): dark curly shoulder-length hair, gold hoop earrings, lavender long-sleeve top, grey joggers.

## 1. SOUND (do this first, with headphones, twice)
Listen to audio-only.mp3 start to finish, then listen to each locked voice, then the video again.
1. Same voice all the way through. Does Skip sound like locked-voice-skip in EVERY line? Dre like locked-voice-dre?
   Flag any line where the pitch, age, accent, speed or "who it is" changes, even slightly. Give the time.
2. Right voice on the right line. Check each line against the script. A line in the wrong character's voice is a BLOCKER.
3. Words. Every word of the script is said, in order, nothing added, nothing swallowed. Names said right ("Dre", not
   "Trey"; "Nia", not "Nya/Mia"). Numbers and prices said right. Compare with transcript-word-times.txt.
4. Delivery. Any line that sounds robotic, flat, rushed, slurred, sing-song, or stressed on the wrong word. Any line
   where the emotion does not fit (a punchline read like a weather report).
5. Glitches. Clicks, pops, buzz, hiss, echo, metallic/underwater sound, a breath or noise cut off, a word clipped at
   its start or end, a line that starts before the last one finished, doubled audio.
6. Levels. Is one character louder than the other? Does any line jump out or drop away? Is anything distorted when
   loud? loudness.txt should read about -14 LUFS (between -15.5 and -13) and true peak at or under -1.0.
7. Silence. silences.txt lists every gap. A gap over 1.5 s that is not a deliberate comedy pause is a problem. Dead
   silence under the end card longer than about 3 s is a problem. The first word must start in the first 0.3 s.
8. Timing of the joke. Is the pause before the punchline long enough to land, and short enough not to drag?

## 2. PICTURE, frame by frame (frames-01.jpg onward, every frame, in order)
For each frame ask all of these:
1. On-model: is every character exactly as described above and exactly like reference-and-stills.jpg? Hair, facial
   hair, clothes, colours, head shape. Flag the first time stamp of anything different.
2. Same person shot to shot: does anyone change between cuts (beard thicker, shirt different, hair different, skin tone)?
3. Hands and bodies: extra or missing fingers, extra arm, floating hand, limb that pops between frames, melting or
   smeared parts, blank eyes, cross-eyes, a face with features missing.
4. Faces up close: patches, specks, stains, a white or coloured blob, a second outline, a broken eye outline, a mouth
   drawn on top of another mouth, a mouth sitting in the wrong place, a mouth that covers the goatee.
5. Mouths and speech: on every line the SPEAKER is on screen and it is the speaker's mouth that moves. Nobody else's
   mouth moves. No line is spoken by a closed mouth. No mouth keeps moving in silence.
6. Text inside the drawing (signs, prices, notes, phone screens, labels): spelled right, says what the script needs,
   the same in every shot, readable, never cut by the edge of the frame, never garbled letters or fake words.
7. Captions: match the spoken words exactly, no missing or extra words, no typos, never over a face, never over
   in-picture text, never cut off at the edges, readable against the background, on screen while the word is said.
8. Hook (first 3 seconds): bold text at top centre from the very first frame; it changes at least every 1.5 s; each
   card makes sense on its own; it does not vanish a frame early or hang over into the next shot.
9. Backgrounds and props: same place, same time of day, same props from shot to shot unless the story moved. A prop
   that changes colour/shape/size, appears or disappears. Anything in the background that looks like a patch, a
   smudge, a blur box or a cover-up.
10. Framing and cuts: speaker cropped badly; a sliver of another character at the frame edge; an unexplained blob at
    an edge; a jump or one-frame flash at a cut; a shot that looks empty or unfinished; picture frozen (no mouth, no
    camera movement) for more than 1.5 s outside the end card.
11. End card: wording right, not over a face, not over a price or sign, long enough to read.
12. Real names and brands: no real store names, logos, magazine titles or product brands in the picture.

## 3. THE JOKE AND THE STORY
1. Would a first-time viewer understand where we are and what is happening within 3 seconds?
2. Is the punchline hidden until its line? (Nothing on screen gives it away early.)
3. Does the last beat land, in picture and in sound? Is it actually funny, or just a statement?
4. Length: target 25-35 s. Say if it drags or feels cut short.
5. Does anything contradict the script (wrong time on a clock, wrong price, wrong prop)?

## 4. POSTING DETAILS (if a title/description is included)
Title leads with the hook, no typos; description has no promises about future videos; any Bible verse is KJV and
quoted exactly; hashtags fit; "made for kids" is No; the AI/altered-content label is on.

## How to report
For every problem: TIME (to the tenth of a second) | SOUND or PICTURE or STORY | what is wrong | BLOCKER / SHOULD FIX / MINOR.
- BLOCKER = wrong or broken: off-model character, wrong voice, wrong or missing words, garbled or wrong text, wrong
  speaker, audio glitch anyone would hear.
- SHOULD FIX = a viewer watching once on a phone would notice it and it looks or sounds like a mistake.
- MINOR = only found by pausing, zooming or replaying, or a matter of taste.
Then list what was checked and found fine, and anything that could not be checked.
Last line, exactly one of:  VERDICT: PASS   or   VERDICT: FAIL
PASS only when there is no BLOCKER and no SHOULD FIX. Do not pass a video because it is better than the last one.
If unsure whether something is a mistake, report it and say so. Never guess that a thing is fine without looking.
