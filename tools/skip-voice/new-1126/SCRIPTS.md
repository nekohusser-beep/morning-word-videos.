## 6. LITTLE SKIP (ADHD): "Two Times on an ADHD Clock", ~30 s
HOOK TEXT: ADHD HAS 2 TIMES: NOW AND NOT NOW
Built on Dr. Russell Barkley's well-known teaching that ADHD is "time blindness": the brain sorts time into "now" and "not now". Paraphrased, credited in the description.

1. (Skip on couch, clock says 11:00) SKIP: Dentist at three. That's four hours. That's basically next week.
2. (Clock 11:01, Skip reorganizes a sock drawer) SKIP: Plenty of time.
3. (Clock 1:30, Skip deep in a video "how pyramids were built") SKIP: Still "not now."
4. (Clock 2:52, alarm, Skip's eyes snap open) SKIP: It's NOW. It's NOW-now.
5. (Skip running out the door with one shoe, toothbrush in mouth)
6. (Receptionist) RECEPTIONIST: Your appointment was yesterday at three.
7. SKIP: ...See, that one was never "now."
END TEXT: Psychologists call it time blindness. Not lazy. 💛
Tip line in desc: set two alarms: one to START getting ready, not one to leave.
Desc seed: "So teach us to number our days" (Psalm 90:12).
